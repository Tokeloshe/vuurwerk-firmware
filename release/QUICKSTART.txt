VUURWERK v1.5.1 - Quick Start

FLASHING

1. Turn the radio off. Hold PTT and turn it on: the flashlight
   LED lights, the bootloader is waiting.
2. Cable in, open https://egzumer.github.io/uvtools/ in Chrome,
   pick vuurwerk-v1.5.1.packed.bin, click Flash firmware.
3. Power cycle. The VUURWERK v1.5.1 banner shows for about a
   second. Channels and settings are kept. See FLASHING.md.

SHORTCUTS (press F, the # key, then the key; toast shown)

F+1     Next band (350 MHz band only when   BAND
        350 En is ON); FREQ ONLY on a
        memory channel
F+2     Switch VFO                          VFO: A  VFO: B
F+3     Frequency / memory mode             VFO MODE  MR MODE
        (NO CHANNELS if none stored)
F+4     Squelch level, wraps 9 to 0         SQL: 0 .. SQL: 9
F+5     Spectrum analyzer                   (no toast)
F+6     TX power                            PWR: LOW  MID  HIGH
F+7     Bandscope on the centre line        SCOPE ON  SCOPE OFF
F+8     Reverse (swap RX and TX)            REVERSE  NORMAL
F+9     Band calling frequency, simplex,    CALL FREQ
        no tones; FREQ ONLY on a memory
        channel, NO BAND where none exists
F+STAR  CTCSS/DCS tone scan                 FM ONLY in AM or USB
F+EXIT  Reset VFO to clean simplex:         SIMPLEX
        offset, tones, scrambler, reverse
        off, FM; FREQ ONLY on a memory
        channel

LONG PRESSES (main screen)

F         Keypad lock on / off         LOCKED  UNLOCKED
STAR      Scan on / off                SCAN ON  SCAN OFF
digit     Same as F + that digit
MENU      Runs the M Long action (Menu > CONFIG > M Long)
EXIT      Cancels a frequency or channel entry

TX

PTT transmits. The bottom line reads TX 0:07 PWR:H (time, power).
Hold SIDE2 while transmitting to send a 1750 Hz tone burst,
whatever SIDE2 is normally bound to.
F+6 cycles TX power. The battery icon keeps updating while you
transmit; a SAG 0.25V toast after the over means the pack sagged
0.20 V or more under load.

MENU

MENU opens the category picker: 1 RECEIVE, 2 TONE, 3 TX, 4 SCAN,
5 CHANNEL, 6 CONFIG. Keys 1-6 jump straight in (1-7 when UNLOCK
is shown, after power-on with PTT+SIDE1 held). UP/DOWN move, MENU
enters or accepts, EXIT backs out. The menu closes after 20 s
without a key. About: MENU, 6, scroll to About, MENU.
Cryptic items show a plain hint on the bottom line while browsing.

INDICATORS

RX S7 -65dBm Q3     receiving: S-level, strength, quality 0-3
TX 0:07 PWR:H       transmitting: elapsed time and power
SCANNING 12c/s      scanning, with the live channels per second
12.5kHz W FM        idle: step, wide or narrow, modulation
Status bar          S = frequency scan, 1 2 * = memory scan list,
                    Cs = tone scan, S+W = Scan+Watch, SP = VFO
                    Split armed, Q bars = signal quality

AUTOMATIC (nothing to set)

Soft key-up and the TX compressor act on every FM transmission.
A CTCSS/DCS lead-in lets the repeater hear your tone first.
Receiver gain adapts to signal strength; the S-meter stays true.
Smart squelch learns the idle noise floor on your frequency.
Squelch tail elimination works on CTCSS and DCS channels (STE).

PATROL (spectrum, VOX mode)

Set it:   STAR until VOX shows. Move to the start of the range,
          press 2 (SET LO). Move to the end, press 8 (SET HI).
Run it:   Press 0. PTRL shows in the corner. 0 or EXIT stops it.
Running:  MENU skips the frequency it is parked on, PTT locks on
          the voice you hear (LOCKED), 0 or EXIT stops. Every
          other key is ignored; on a dark screen the first key
          only wakes the backlight.
Settings: Menu > SCAN: PSens LOW/NORM/HIGH, PSpeed SLOW/NORM,
          PDwell SHORT/NORM/LONG, PResum SHORT/NORM/LONG.
It stops on voice, and also on idle carriers, birdies and data
tones. A 60 s cap moves it on and skips that frequency for about
2 minutes. With no range set it walks the span on screen.
At 5 % battery PATROL stops and LOW BATTERY shows.
The range survives power cycles and Reset.

VOX LOCK CARRY-OVER (EXIT while locked, frequency mode)

The VFO takes the locked frequency, rounded to the VFO step, and
the spectrum's modulation. It keeps its own offset and tones: if
+, -, CT or DCS show after a simplex find, press F+EXIT first.

SPECTRUM SIDE BUTTONS

SIDE1 / SIDE2   Move the span up / down (repeat while held)
MENU            Blacklist the current peak
In the STILL view (PTT in NORM or PEAK): SIDE1 = monitor,
SIDE2 = backlight, EXIT = back to the sweep.
While PATROL runs the side keys are ignored.

CSS SCANNER (F+STAR, FM only)

Press F+STAR while the station is transmitting.
You see:   NO SIGNAL (dead air), SCAN CMP. (found) or SCAN FAIL.
           (nothing in 30 s, double beep).
Save it:   MENU, then MENU again at SAVE? (RX and TX tone). On a
           memory channel you first pick the channel number, then
           MENU again.
Menu scan: in RxCTCS or RxDCS press MENU, then STAR to scan;
           MENU accepts the found tone. Your offset, TX tone and
           power are untouched, so you can key up straight away.
