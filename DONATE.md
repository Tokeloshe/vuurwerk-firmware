## 3. Support without spending a cent

These matter more than you'd think:

- **Star the repo.** Stars are how other UV-K5 owners find VUURWERK.
- **Share it.** A post in your radio club, forum, or subreddit reaches
  operators who will never see a GitHub page.
- **Send field reports.** Real-world observations from real radios drive the
  best fixes in this firmware. Some of VUURWERK's most important improvements
  started as a single operator taking the time to describe exactly what their
  radio did.
